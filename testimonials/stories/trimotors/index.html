The Journey

Challenge → Transformation → Impact

! The Challenge

A leads team doing data entry instead of selling

TriMotors sells the Bajaj RE, Maxima Z and Maxima Cargo across the Philippines. Its leads team spent the day moving information between tabs rather than turning inquiries into applications a dealer could close.

  • Information typed by hand from one tab or Excel sheet to the next
  • The team focused on manual tasks instead of closing
  • Reports tedious to generate
  • No way to compare who performed better month to month
⇄ The Transformation

Google Sheets and multiple apps into one hub

Rework fit what the leads team needed at a price that worked. The first phase was genuinely hard — the interface was unfamiliar — and Rework's CS team worked through it with them until go-live.

  • Google Sheets and multiple apps replaced by one hub
  • Onboarding tough at first, smooth by go-live — two months end to end
  • Real-time reports instead of manual assembly
  • A leads funnel that keeps convertible customers in view
✓ The Impact

Nearly 3× the volume, with the attribution to prove it

Between May and July the team's output scaled sharply — and marketing can now show concrete evidence of an inquiry travelling from a Meta ad through to a sale.

  • Purchase applications 27 → 78 per month (May → July)
  • Units sold up 44% — 9 to 13 per month, same window
  • Team performance comparable month to month
  • Meta ad inquiries traceable through to sales conversion
