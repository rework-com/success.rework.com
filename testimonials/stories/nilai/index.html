The Journey

Challenge → Transformation → Impact

! The Challenge

A CRM that fought the admissions team

On a basic Salesforce plan, just five seats were shared across a 15-person team — and everything went in by hand.

  • Only 5 user seats shared by a team of 15
  • Every lead & contact typed in manually
  • Batching updates to save time meant leads went missing
  • Reports never tallied — hours spent reconciling figures
⇄ The Transformation

More seats, less spend, real support

A social post caught the boss's eye. The pricing and included features won it — then the onboarding sealed it.

  • 15 accounts — up from 5, for less than before
  • Features included, not paywalled tier-by-tier
  • Onboarding in person + online, retrained until it clicked
  • A team that actually responds — even late at night
✓ The Impact

From hours to minutes — numbers you can trust

Updates that used to take hours now take minutes, and month-end reports finally reconcile on their own.

  • Lead & record updates: hours → minutes
  • Daily, weekly & monthly reports reconcile automatically
  • Numbers leadership can trust at a glance
  • Fewer missed leads — updates happen in the moment
