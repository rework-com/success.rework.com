! The Challenge

Three systems in, still losing the database

ASK HRD runs on client relationships — nine years of training, profiling and advisory work. But the meetings and conversations that carry that business had nowhere reliable to live.

  • 3 management systems tried — none of them stuck
  • Client records lost between meetings
  • Status reports talked up by hand — claimed effort, negative results
  • Income lost to targets that were missed but never flagged
⇄ The Transformation

A system nobody was afraid to touch

Dr. Adeva found Rework through its social presence. What made it land was an onboarding that took the fear out of the software — "try it, press all the buttons you want."

  • Their own data loaded in first — the dashboard showed where the work was really going

  • Pipelines built to match how they actually sell
