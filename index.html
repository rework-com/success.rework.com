3–5 days
Design & Finetune
Discover your current process, map it to a digital-ready workflow, get sign-off before we build anything.
2
3–5 days

Configure your system end-to-end — pipelines, automations, integrations, permissions, data migration.
✓ Pilot gate
3

4
~5 days

Pipeline design doesn't match how your team actually sells
We start by reviewing how your team handles 5 recent deals — pipeline stages are confirmed before we build, not after.
Self-service data migration stalls on encoding, dates, or duplicates
We provide validated templates plus a 30-min Q&A slot during setup. You import, we check for issues before go-live.
Low adoption after launch — team reverts to spreadsheets
If activity falls below 70% by Month 3, you get 1 free targeted training session plus an adoption health-check report — at no extra cost.
Process consulting reveals you don't have a formal sales process
We use Rework's standard sales framework as the starting point, then customize from there. No judgment — this is common.
Data migration finds mismatches, duplicates, or missing fields
CS-handled migration includes a deduplication and validation pass. We surface issues before go-live, not after.
Lead routing rules don't match how your reps actually share territory
Pioneer Team pilot in Week 3–4 catches routing misfits before company-wide rollout. 1 free adjustment cycle included.
Role-based training creates inconsistency between admins, managers, and reps
Workshops are sequenced (admins first, then managers, then reps) so each layer reinforces the next. Custom guide covers the same workflow from each role's view.
Low adoption after launch

Stakeholder misalignment across teams or regions
P1 stakeholder interviews surface conflicts before configuration. Discovery Summary forces a single management sign-off before build begins.
Multi-source data migration finds incompatible field definitions
Migration is sequenced — each source mapped and validated before merging. Deduplication catches conflicts. Issues surface at mid-point review, not at go-live.
Custom dashboard requirements drift during build
Dashboard scope signed off upfront. Changes after sign-off go to a separate Dashboard & Analytics engagement.
Train-the-trainer underperforms when internal champions aren't given time
Champions nominated with a written time commitment. We coach them through pilot. Escalation path defined if commitment isn't kept.
50+ user adoption is uneven across teams or regions

Executive expectations exceed what's in scope

FAQ
Common questions
What's the difference between the three packages?
Launchpad is for small teams getting started — standard configuration, self-service data migration, one group training workshop. Accelerator adds process consulting, Rework-handled migration with validation, role-based training, and 30 days of post-go-live group chat. Transform is a full redesign for complex orgs — multi-pipeline/multi-team configuration, custom dashboards, train-the-trainer, a dedicated Onboarding Manager, and 60 days of dedicated CS engagement. Use "Find my package" to get a tailored recommendation.
Who should we pick as our project lead (Rework Master)?
We strongly recommend nominating a single project lead on your side — we call this person your Rework Master. Ideally they:

· Understand your business — they know how your team actually works today
· Have decision-making authority — they can approve scope and resolve cross-team conflicts
· Are excited about new tech — they want to make Rework work, not resist change

During implementation, the Rework Master partners with our CS team to drive the project. After go-live, they become your internal expert — the person teammates ask when they have a Rework question. Without one, implementation still runs, but adoption is slower and decisions get bottlenecked.
What's the Customer Health Check?
After go-live, your CS team sends a quarterly active-usage report showing daily-active rate by team. If the rate drops below the healthy threshold (50% of named users active on a typical business day), we book a review call to identify what's blocking adoption — at no extra cost. Same commitment for every tier across every product.
How long does implementation actually take?
Timeline depends on product and tier. For Sales Ops, Work Ops, People Ops: Launchpad 3–5 weeks · Accelerator 4–8 weeks · Transform 8–12 weeks. Lead Ops is faster: Launchpad 2–4 weeks · Accelerator 4–6 weeks · Transform 6–10 weeks. Your CS team sets a clear timeline at kick-off and tracks it through go-live. Actual duration depends on your team's responsiveness, data complexity, and scope.
Can I add or change services after implementation?
Yes. Professional Services (Dashboard & Analytics, Integration, Training & Adoption, Sales Consulting, AI Agents) and Add-ons (Onsite Delivery, Pre-implementation Readiness) can be added at any point, before or after go-live. Upgrading your core package mid-implementation is possible but handled case-by-case with your CS team, since the scope needs to be re-aligned.
